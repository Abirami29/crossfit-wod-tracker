# Benchmark time/score sources

Bands used in `workouts.json` (`benchmark_bands`) for Fran, Grace, Cindy, Diane, Murph, DT, and Kalsu were derived from consensus across multiple independent CrossFit coaching/community sources, cross-checked against each other (not a single source, not invented). Representative sources per workout:

- **Fran**: CrossFit.com, WODBuilders, marathonhandbook.com, WODtime Calculator - consistent beginner ~7-10min, intermediate ~5-7min, advanced ~3-5min, elite sub-3min.
- **Grace**: CrossFit.com, BarBend, WODtime Calculator, fitmixonline - beginner ~5-8min, intermediate ~3-5min, advanced ~2-3min, elite sub-2min.
- **Cindy**: ritfitsports, WODBuilders, wodtimecalculator, getfitl - beginner 8-13 rounds, intermediate 14-18, advanced 19-22, elite 23+.
- **Diane**: WODprep, anabelavila, drworkout.fitness, nonstopcrossfit - beginner ~10-14min, intermediate ~6-9min, advanced ~4-6min, elite sub-4min.
- **Murph**: velitessport, WODprep, marathonhandbook, CrossFit.com - beginner ~60-80min, intermediate ~45-58min, advanced ~35-45min, elite sub-35min.
- **DT**: marathonhandbook, WODBuilders, forgetheflow, myherowod - beginner ~15-20min, intermediate ~9-15min, advanced ~6-9min, elite sub-6min.
- **Kalsu**: BOXROX (multiple articles), consistent across all - beginner 22-30min, intermediate 19-22min, advanced 15-19min, elite sub-14min.

## Why CrossFit Open workouts (25.1-26.3) have no benchmark bands

Open workouts are scored via the official worldwide leaderboard (percentile rank among that year's submissions), not via descriptive "good time" community writeups the way long-standing benchmark WODs are. There's no equivalent consensus "beginner/intermediate/advanced" time band publicly available for a given Open workout the way there is for Fran or Murph. Rather than fabricate plausible-sounding numbers, these workouts rely on personal-best comparison only, once an athlete has logged at least one attempt.

## Caveat

These are still community-sourced approximate ranges, not a peer-reviewed or standardized data set - different coaching sites vary somewhat in exact minute cutoffs, and none of this accounts for weight class, scaling choices, or equipment access. Treat these as reasonable reference points for a demo, not authoritative performance standards.
